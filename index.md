   ## biography
   Rob is pursuing a Master of International Affairs at Columbia University, concentrating in Data Science for Policy.  He earned a bachelor’s in Political Economy from UC Berkeley, an MPP from the University of Michigan, and an MS in Financial Mathematics from the University of Chicago.  
   
   Rob has been a meat cutter, amateur boxer, filmmaker, and organizer.  Today, he is developing option3 – a new media think tank addressing policy and culture around social cohesion in late modernity - and preparing to earn a social science PhD.  He is also active in platelet donation, prison volunteering, and mental health advocacy.
