   ## biography
    Rob is pursuing a Master of International Affairs at Columbia University’s School of International and Public Affairs, concentrating in Data Science for Policy. He earned a bachelor’s in Political Economy from UC Berkeley, an MPP from the University of Michigan, and an MS in Financial Mathematics from the University of Chicago.  Having been a meat cutter, filmmaker, and organizer, he is developing option3 – a new media think tank addressing policy and culture around social cohesion in late modernity - and aims to earn a social science PhD.  Rob is active in platelet donation, prison volunteering, and mental health advocacy.
